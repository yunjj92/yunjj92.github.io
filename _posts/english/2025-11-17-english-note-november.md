## New Words & Expressions

### 1. refer to
- **Part of Speech**: verb
- **Synonym**: consult, look up,
  - In documentation: reference, cite, cross-reference, point to
  - In engineering domains: use as a reference, refer back to, check, look at
  - In academic fields: cite, draw on, consult
- **Definition**:
  - consult: to get information or advice from the person, book, etc. with special knowledge of a particular subject.
  - look up: to try to find a piece of information by looking in a book or on a computer
  - draw on: to use information or your knowledge of something to help you do something. 
- **My example**:
  > "If you don't know what the word means, you look it up in a dictionary."
  > "I'm not quite sure how to get there. I'd better consult a map"
  > "His novels draw heavily on his childhood."
- **Notes**:
  - draw on/upon something: to start to using a supply of something
    > "Americans spend more than they earn, drawing on saving and building up dept to do so."
  - draw someone into something: to make someone become involved in a difficult or unpleasant situation.
    > "They're trying to draw me into the argument. But I refused."

### 2. exfiltrate(ˈɛksfɪlˌtreɪt)
- **Part of Speech**: verb
- **Definition**: 
  - (military) to remove or withdraw (an intelligent agent, soldier, etc.) surreptitiously from an enemy-held area
  - (sometimes passive) to remove (data) from computer, network, etc. surreptitiously or without permission unlawfully
  - (in American) to escape furtively from an area under enemy control.
- **Example from source**:
  > "Letting Claude run arbitrary commands is risky and can result in data loss, system corruption, or even data exfilteration."
- **My example**:
  > "Cyvercriminals exfilterate data in just three days"
  > "New malware samples exfilterate WhatsApp data to target Iran regime's enemies."
- **Notes**:
  - furtively /ˈfɜː.tɪv.li/: secretly and often dishonestly

### 3. surreptitiously
